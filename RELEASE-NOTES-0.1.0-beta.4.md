# Woven Oak v0.1.0-beta.4 — Rough Beta

**Status: ROUGH BETA** — expect rough edges, and please report
them. Testers: phone model, Android version, what worked, what
broke, via GitHub Issues.

Two upgrades to Chant mode, both directed by Paul during the
beta.3 field test:

## The words fit the music now

In beta.3 your phrases started on the beat but kept their
natural length. Now every phrase is digitally stretched or
squeezed to **exactly one beat** before it lands — say it slow,
say it fast, it comes out riding the tempo. The anchor refrain
is fitted the same way.

## Four styles to pour your words into

Tap **🤖 Chant…** and choose a style — described, never named
for an artist, each one an original synthesized recipe:

- **Robot Chant** — four-on-the-floor pulse, ring-modulated
  robot voice. 120 BPM.
- **Acid House** — driving kick and a squelchy bass arp.
  124 BPM.
- **Industrial Drive** — heavy driven kicks, metallic hits, a
  low drone, crushed voice. 100 BPM.
- **Dark Synth** — kick-and-snare groove, pulsing bass, your
  voice left clean. 105 BPM.

The tempo slider still lets you move any style off its default.
Every word in every chant is still the user's own — the
templates ship empty, and everything stays on-device with one
permission: the microphone.

## Install

Same signing key as beta.2 and beta.3 — installs straight over
the top.
