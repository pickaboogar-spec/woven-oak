## New: Retune — sing it, then tune it

Stage 1 of the voice plan (see VOICE-PLAN.md) is here. Record
yourself singing on a pad, tap **🎯 Retune…**, and pick:

- a **key** (C through B),
- a **scale** — Major, Minor, or Chromatic,
- a **strength** — how hard your notes are pulled to the
  scale, from a gentle nudge to the full robotic snap.

The app reads your take with its own pitch tracker and
re-renders it at the corrected pitch, right on the phone. It
always starts from your original take, so you can re-run it at
a different strength whenever you like — nothing is ever lost,
and nothing leaves the device.

This is the foundation the rest of the voice plan stands on:
the same pitch shifter will voice the calibration samples
when your own voice becomes an instrument.
