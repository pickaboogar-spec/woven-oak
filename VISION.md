# Woven Oak — The Bigger Vision (Paul, 2026-11-07, in his words as spoken)

Where the pads and the chant are heading, as Paul laid it out
the night beta.5 shipped. Future direction, not yet built —
recorded so it isn't lost.

- **Call your drums into being.** When someone selects a beat,
  they should be able to *say* it: "make a drum sound like a
  clap on metal," or "on plastic" — describe the drum, and the
  program synthesizes that drum.
- **Hum the instruments.** Hum strings, hum air instruments;
  the program converts the hum into those instruments (the
  hum-to-instrument already in the app is the seed of this).
- **Compose from the pieces.** The program puts the parts
  together into a unique musical composition the player can
  play with and work with until it's actually great.
- **Singing becomes an instrument.** When somebody sings, the
  app records their voice AND the notes they hit, and can
  duplicate their voice for future sounds.
- **A calibration song.** A sample lyric somebody sings that
  walks all the notes they need to hit — mapping their vocal
  range — and captures the character of their actual voice, so
  later, if they type words in, it can sing them back *in their
  voice*, as long as they've created the music for it.
- **Real music theory underneath.** There has to be a chorus
  and a bridge and all of those things — the app helps people
  determine where those come into play, so by playing around
  with their phone they create actual music people will enjoy
  listening to.
