# Woven Oak v0.1.0-beta.8 — Rough Beta

**Status: ROUGH BETA** — expect rough edges, and please report
them. Testers: phone model, Android version, what worked, what
broke, via GitHub Issues.

## New: the beauty pass

Same instrument, finished furniture. The six pads are bubbles
now — big, soft-rounded, rimmed with a hairline of light and
lifted gently off the screen. They still wear their state
colors (red while recording, green while looping, breathing
while they play), so you read the board exactly as before —
it just looks like somebody was paid to care.

Every action button is rounded to match, and the flat black
background is gone: the app sits in a deep green-charcoal
gradient, dark as oak bark at the edges.

Nothing moved and nothing was renamed. Retune, Chant, Sounds,
Hum and Export all work exactly as they did in beta.7.

## Releases

Per the project's release policy, only the two most recent
versions stay downloadable: beta.8 (current) and beta.7
(fallback). Beta.6 is retired with this release. Same signing
key throughout — beta.8 installs straight over the top.
