# Woven Oak v0.1.0-beta.1 — Rough Beta

First public beta of Woven Oak, a truly electronic acapella
instrument: six pads on your Android phone. Sing, hum, beatbox or
clap a short phrase into a pad and it loops — layer the six pads and
you are your own choir, band and rhythm section.

**This is a ROUGH BETA.** Expect rough edges.

## What's in it

- Six loop pads: tap to record, tap again to stop — the loop starts
  playing automatically. Tap a playing pad to stop it; long-press
  to clear it. Play All / Stop All for the whole piece.
- 30 built-in instrument voices, synthesized by the app itself
  (nothing sampled, completely license-clean): hum a melody, pick a
  voice, and your hum comes back as that instrument.
- Live pitch display while you sing or hum: the note you are on
  (sharps and flats), how far off it you are, and your vocal range
  for the session.
- Gift a loop: export any pad's loop as a WAV file and share it —
  creation is the best gift.
- One permission only: the microphone. Everything happens on your
  device; recordings never leave your phone. No accounts, no
  telemetry, no ads. Free forever, GPL v3.

## Install

Download `WovenOak-0.1.0-beta.1.apk` (attached to this release),
open it on your phone, and allow the install. Requires Android 8.0
(API 26) or newer. The APK is debug-signed, so Android may warn that
it comes from outside the Play Store — that is normal for a beta
like this.

## Testers wanted
