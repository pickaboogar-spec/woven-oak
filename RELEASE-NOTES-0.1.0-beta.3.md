## New: Chant mode — a music-creation template

Paul's design, drawn up and built the same evening (see
CHANT-TEMPLATE.md). The idea: imitate the *mechanism* of the
classic robot-chant records — a driving electronic pulse, a
processed voice chanting short phrases one to a beat, phrases
stacking until the list becomes the song — while every word
stays **your own**. No preset lyrics, no borrowed hook; the
template ships empty.

How it works:

1. Record short phrases onto at least two pads — a verb and a
   word or two each ("chop the onions", "stir the pot", whatever
   your world gives you).
2. Tap **🤖 Chant… build a robot chant from my pads**.
3. Choose a tempo (90–140 BPM), optionally pick an **anchor
   pad** — its phrase lands every four beats as the refrain —
   and leave the electronic pulse and robot voice on, or not.
4. **Build & play.** First pass runs half your phrases, second
   pass runs them all, over the pulse. Stop all stops the chant.

Everything is still on-device. Still one permission: the
microphone.
